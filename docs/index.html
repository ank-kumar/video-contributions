What lands here next

I have thousands of clips going back years, most of them never published. They are being catalogued by trip, place and date, and will appear here as they are processed.

The files themselves will live on the Internet Archive and, where the footage is useful to an encyclopaedia, on Wikimedia Commons. This page is the index, not the warehouse.
